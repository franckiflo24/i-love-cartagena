# AMO Life / AMO Cartagena — Forward Readiness & Coverage Audit (Cartagena de Indias, Colombia) · Oct 2026 → Oct 2027

The near-term calendar is now mostly lockable: the Alcaldía/IPCC published the full Fiestas de Independencia 2026 agenda on Sept 16, 2026 (Bando Nov 12, Festival Náutico Nov 13–14 with Juan Luis Guerra, Nicky Jam, Silvestre Dangond and others, Reinado Popular coronation Nov 15),\[1\]\[2\] and Cartagena Festival de Música (Jan 9–17, 2027), Hay Festival (Jan 28–31, 2027)\[3\] and FICCI 66 (Apr 6–11, 2027) all have organizer-confirmed dates\[3\]\[4\]\[5\] — but four items still cannot be confirmed from public sources as of Oct 7, 2026: the Señorita Colombia 2026 coronation date, the 2026 Christmas-lights switch-on date, the Festival del Pastel 2026 venue, and any public footprint at all for Cartagena Music Week (CMW).

## TL;DR

- **Lock now (Oct–Feb):** Load the official Fiestas de Independencia agenda (Oct 2 – Nov 16, 2026) immediately, plus CONFIRMED Festival de Música (Jan 9–17), Hay Festival (Jan 28–31, tickets open in November) and the Candelaria/Frito window (typically Jan 24–Feb 2); the Festival Náutico is free but sells out via third-party boat packages (COP 750,000–950,000 per person/day, yachts COP 5–42 million/day), so boat inventory — not tickets — is the bookable product.
- **Biggest gap is AMO's own flagship:** No public announcement, website, social channel, lineup or ticketing for "Cartagena Music Week" (Dec 31, 2026 – Jan 7, 2027) could be found on the open web. The brief's 2–4-month US booking lead time has no source we could find (Expedia's 2026 Air Hacks Report puts the cheapest US international fares at 8–15 days out and the most expensive at 180+ days out), but CMW content and a "notify me"/PALCO RSVP page should still go live by mid-October 2026 to catch early international planners. It must also be clearly separated from the classical Cartagena Festival de Música, which starts two days after CMW ends.
- **Demand/cruise:** The 2026–27 cruise season (started Sept 10, 2026) has 173 scheduled calls from 37 lines over 152 operating days with ~416,050 projected visitors and 47 multi-ship days (36 double, 11 triple); combine with the Dec 15–Jan 15 peak, Semana Santa 2027 (Palm Sunday Mar 21 – Easter Mar 28) and the June–July school break for crowd forecasting — note conflicting official counts (171 calls/35 lines in a later report).

## Key Findings

1. **Fiestas de Independencia 2026 is CONFIRMED and detailed** — the Alcaldía/IPCC presented the official agenda on Sept 16, 2026 at the Centro de Convenciones;\[1\] programming runs from the Canapote prelude on Oct 2 to the Reinado Intercorregimental on Nov 16.\[6\] An earlier February (ANATO) version differs on several dates — the September version supersedes it.
2. **Señorita Colombia 2026 coronation: still TYPICAL-WINDOW-TBC.** The official Fiestas agenda lists only "Entrega de llaves a las candidatas del Concurso Nacional de Belleza" on Mon Nov 9 (Baluarte San Miguel, "por confirmar").\[2\] Historical pattern (coronation on the Monday Independence holiday: Nov 17, 2025; Nov 10, 2024)\[7\]\[8\] points to **Monday Nov 16, 2026**, but no organizer confirmation was found.
3. **Christmas lights 2026: TYPICAL-WINDOW-TBC.** The IPCC 2026 agenda says only "en diciembre … encendido de la Super Navidad."\[9\] Pattern: Fri Dec 6, 2024 and Fri Dec 5, 2025 (7:00 p.m., Plaza de la Aduana)\[10\]\[11\] → most likely **Fri Dec 4, 2026**.
4. **Festival del Pastel 2026: dates ANNOUNCED, venue TBC, with a source conflict** — the Alcaldía's Jan 2, 2026 agenda says Dec 17–26;\[9\] a secondary summary says Dec 17–27.\[12\] The 2025 edition moved to the new Plaza de Variedades (Parque Espíritu del Manglar) and ran Dec 17–27.\[13\]
5. **CMW: not publicly discoverable.** This is the single most urgent readiness gap.
6. **FICCI 66 is CONFIRMED for Apr 6–11, 2027** (organizer X account and Festhome call),\[5\]\[14\] resolving the earlier "~Apr 6–11" item.
7. **Ticketing reality:** most Cartagena anchors are free/public (Fiestas, Náutico, Pastel, Frito, Música Sacra, alumbrado) — the app's PALCO free-RSVP rail fits these; paid ticketing is concentrated in Festival de Música (own site, national/international card checkout), Hay Festival (own box office, opens November), private boat packages for the Náutico, and commercial concerts on TuBoleta.
