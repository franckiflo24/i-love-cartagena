# PART A — THE COMPLETE TOURIST ACTIVITY MAP (Stage-1 enumeration spine)

## 1. Neighborhoods & Zones
- **Centro Histórico (Walled City)**: UNESCO core — **Centro** (main hub: cathedrals, plazas, boutique hotels, fine dining) + **San Diego** (quieter, craft-cocktail lounges).
- **Getsemaní**: bohemian heart — street art (Afro-Colombian identity), Plaza de la Trinidad (street beers ~7pm), Calle Media Luna + Calle del Arsenal nightlife. Forbes "coolest neighborhoods in the world" (2018).
- **Bocagrande**: modern high-rise beach zone ("Miami"), casinos, Av. San Martín dining.
- **Castillogrande & El Laguito**: upscale peninsular, quieter beaches, luxury condos.
- **Manga**: island neighborhood, cruise terminal (Sociedad Portuaria), longer stays.
- **La Boquilla**: Afro-Colombian fishing village — mangrove canoe tours, kitesurfing.
- **Crespo** (airport, budget beachfront) · **Marbella** (local beach) · **Cabrero, Pie de la Popa**.

## 2. Landmarks & History
Castillo San Felipe de Barajas (largest Spanish colonial fortress in the Americas; withstood Vernon's 1741 siege — 186 ships/~25,000 men) · Torre del Reloj + Plaza de los Coches + Portal de los Dulces · Las Bóvedas (dungeons → artisan market) · Palacio de la Inquisición (tribunal from 1610, ~800 punished/executed; museum) · Cartagena Cathedral (16th c.) · San Pedro Claver ("slave of the slaves") · Santo Domingo Church · La Popa Convent (best views) · Plaza de Bolívar (+ Museo del Oro Zenú) · Plaza Santo Domingo (Botero's Gertrudis) · Plaza de San Diego · Plaza de la Aduana · Plaza de la Trinidad · Las Murallas (~11km; Café del Mar sunset) · Getsemaní murals (Calle de la Sierpe, Callejón Angosto) · Teatro Adolfo Mejía.

## 3. Islands & Beaches
Rosario Islands (45min–1hr speedboat; Corales del Rosario national park; day-pass clubs **Mambo, Cocoliso, Rosario del Mar/Tequendama**; oceanarium; bioluminescence) · Isla Grande · Barú/Playa Blanca · **Cholón** (party island) · Tierra Bomba (ATV, San Fernando fort, day passes) · Bocagrande/Castillogrande in-city beaches · La Boquilla. Departures: **Muelle de la Bodeguita** (~US$30–106 shared → private charters).

## 4. Food — Culinary Map
- **Iconic dishes**: posta negra cartagenera · arroz con coco (titoté) · cazuela de mariscos · pescado frito con patacones · arepa de huevo · carimañola · mote de queso · sancocho de pescado · ceviche · empanadas cartageneras · arroz con carne en tabaquito · cocadas (palenqueras) · fruit from palenqueras.
- **Drinks**: limonada de coco · raspao · corozo/mango/guanábana juices · aguardiente · agua de panela.
- **Fine dining**: Celele (Jaime Rodríguez, Proyecto Caribe Lab) · Carmen · La Vitrola · Interno · Alma (Casa San Agustín) · Don Juan · **Leo** (Leonor Espinosa) · 1621 (Sofitel Santa Clara) · La Cocina de Pepina · La Mulata · La Cevichería (Bourdain) · **Casa Mar** · **Agua de León** · **Román**.
- **Street/markets**: **Mercado de Bazurto** (food tours) · **Donde Magola** · Donde Olano · **Puesto de Cecilia** (Bazurto) · Portal de los Dulces.
- **Experiences**: cooking classes (Bazurto runs), food tours, chocolate/rum/coffee tastings, private chefs.

## 5. Nightlife

## 10. Weddings & MICE

## 11. Practical Traveler Intelligence
- **Air**: Rafael Núñez (CTG) — 7,510,922 pax 2024 (record, +16%; intl 1,671,204 +22%), 7,756,298 in 2025; 3rd-busiest in Colombia; OINAC concession since Mar 2024; nonstops: MIA, FLL, JFK, ATL, YYZ/YUL, PTY, MEX (Feb 2025), MAD, AMS; COP ~1.4T expansion, new ~17,600m² intl terminal.
- **Cruise**: Manga terminal ~3km/10–15min from Old City (free shuttle to Puerta del Reloj).
- **Getting around**: taxis have NO meters — agree price first; InDrive/Uber/DiDi/Cabify safer default (esp. after midnight); water taxis; Transcaribe BRT; horse carriages being phased to electric.
- **Safety**: petty theft/phone snatching, taxi overcharge, aggressive vendors, fake tours, bait-and-switch menus, rare scopolamine drink-spiking. Violent crime vs tourists uncommon in main zones. Safe: Centro, San Diego, Getsemaní main streets, Bocagrande, Castillogrande. Tourist police wear green.
- **Timing**: dry Dec–Apr; value May & Nov; rainy/cheap Aug–Oct.
- **Money**: ~10% propina voluntaria often added; small cash; ask prices first; no tap water.
- **Nomads**: Getsemaní/Centro cafés, coworking, digital-nomad visa.

## 12. Who Travels
US #1 source (~24% national) · regional LatAm · growing UK/NL/ES · large domestic base · cruise day-trippers (majority US, 60%+ aged 60+). Segments: luxury (villas/yachts/weddings) · backpackers · bachelor(ette)/groups · nomads · cruise 6–8hr loops · families · MICE.

# PART B — MARKET & ECONOMIC CASE (deck evidence — re-verify per caveats)

## Volume & Growth
Colombia: 6,696,835 non-resident visitors 2024 (+8.5%); forex US$10.220bn 2024 (+14%, ANATO) → ~US$11.166bn 2025; 2025 intl movements >10.2M (+6%) = #1 South America. Cartagena: >855k intl 2024; ~930k Dec24–Jan25 season → record ~1.6M Dec25–Jan26 (+5%); FY24 occupancy 73%; STR: median ADR ~US$93–104, Old City/Getsemaní US$120–180+, RevPAR pressure 2025.

## Cruise Economy
~97% of Colombia's cruise pax (34 lines). 23-24: ~327,434 pax/188 calls. 24-25 target ~402,848 (~US$42.6M). **25-26 actual: 476,666 visitors, 175 calls, ~US$59M** (record). Per-pax spend: ~US$122.5 transit, ~US$71 crew, ~US$280 home-port (BREA). Q1 2026 national: 174,371 pax/103 calls; Cartagena 80 calls/158k+ movements.

## Economic Impact
National: tourism ≈3.9–5% GDP (~US$19–21bn), ~900k direct jobs; forex surpassed coffee and coal, ~86% of oil-export value Q1 2025. Bolívar: highest avg daily spend per intl visitor (~US$211.5 MinCIT; conservative ~US$120–150 Semana/Corpoturismo). Accommodation+food ≈10% of Cartagena employment; tourism touches 40%+ of city jobs; ~70–75% of Bolívar economy = Cartagena.

## Digital Context
DataReportal 2025: 41.1M internet (77.3%), 36.8M social (69.2%), 96.6% mobile; WhatsApp near-universal, cheapest LatAm biz-messaging (~US$0.0008/msg); smartphones → ~44M by 2029. Current tourist stack: Google Maps, TripAdvisor, Instagram, Viator/GetYourGuide, GuruWalk, hotel concierges, PDF guides. Gap: curated + trusted + real-time + conversational + gamified.

## Competitive Landscape
OTAs (Viator/GetYourGuide/Civitatis/Expedia — transactional) · GuruWalk (+AI planner plug-in; guide-centric) · official portals (static) · TripAdvisor/GMaps (crowd-sourced, gameable) · luxury human concierges (not scalable) · generic AI concierges (not Cartagena-curated). **Whitespace: AI-native, locally-curated, trust-scored, gamified passport walking-layer concierge — nobody owns the combination.**

## Tech & Investment Climate
LatAm's 3rd startup ecosystem (2,126 startups 2025, +24%, KPMG/iNNpulsa); 2024 VC ~US$354M (Cuantico) to ~US$513M (KPMG), fintech-skewed, Bogotá 80–89% of capital; FDI US$14.2bn 2024; early-stage +55% 2025; LatAm VC ~US$4.1bn 2025. Government: "Colombia, the Country of Beauty"; tourism as "new engine"; MinCIT/ProColombia/Corpoturismo/MinTIC alignment.

## Investor Angle
Category: "first AI-native city concierge for a country." TAM: 855k+ intl + ~477k cruise + millions domestic → multi-million-user funnel. Monetization: commissions, premium tiers, B2B venue analytics, B2G dashboards. Comparables: Rappi, travel-tech, AI-concierge entrants. Defensibility: curated catalog + trust layer + gamification + WhatsApp-native distribution — compounds with usage.
