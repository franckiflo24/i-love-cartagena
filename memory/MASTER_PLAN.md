# THE THESIS

AMO stops being "a travel app" and becomes **the operating system for experiencing a place** — first Cartagena, then Colombia. Three loops, interlocking:

1. **The Tourist Loop** — walk the city, the city talks back, your passport fills, you share it, your friends download it.
2. **The Business Loop** — venues see the foot traffic AMO drives, pay to be seen more, feed live content (pulse) that makes the tourist loop richer.
3. **The Data Loop** — every search, tap, walk-by, and check-in becomes intelligence nobody else has: sellable to partners as marketing, to the city as tourism policy, and back into Luna as smarter answers.

Each loop feeds the other two. That's the machine.

**What exists (verified live as of docking):** 872 venues, owned images, place-verified coords (161 corrected via Place IDs Aug 3) · Luna (Sonnet, intent-routed, occasion tags, signature dishes, knowledge spine) · walking layer (nearby strip, compass, gem reveals) · Mi Pasaporte (20 grounded plates, 12 plazas, streaks, 75m server-verified check-ins, share card + OG unfurl route) · LIVE map (real-time tracking dot, distances, gold Pasaporte filter, 6th tab) · Locals Recommend (curated + behavioral gate) · /intel (demand mining, leads, divergence) · partner pulse (code-complete, awaiting 3 Meta console values) · behavioral ranking (CTR loop) · 4 languages · business portal · auto version-stamp deploys.

# PHASE 1 — THE ADDICTION LAYER, COMPLETED (Weeks 1–4)
*Goal: a tourist who opens AMO once keeps it open all trip and shows it to their table.*

**1.1 Quests & Trails (Drop 5 — the engine)**
- Neighborhood trails: "Ruta Getsemaní" (7 stops: murals, gem, plaza, arepa), "Sabores del Centro" (5 plates), "Atardecer Perfecto" (rooftop → walls → sunset cruise).
- **Cruise Mode**: onboarding already flags cruise passengers → auto-offer "4 Horas en Cartagena," time-boxed, walkable from the pier, redemption at the finish. 250K+ passengers/season = the wedge audience.
- Daily quest: "Hoy: prueba una limonada de coco" — one per day, value-tied, never nagging.

**1.2 Redemptions (the reward economy — partner-funded, no tokens)**
- Complete a trail → "show this screen" at a partner → free arepa / welcome cocktail / 2x1.
- Redemption codes logged → partner sees exactly how many customers AMO delivered (this number becomes the Phase 2 sales pitch).

**1.3 The Pulse goes LIVE** *(dependency: the 3 Meta console values — the one human unlock this phase needs)*

**1.4 Push that respects the user (web now, native later)**
- PWA install prompt + opt-in push for *your* stuff only (streak milestone, quest complete, redemption expiring). Frequency cap: max 1/day. Every push is value, never guilt. No proximity spam on web.

**1.5 The referral loop**
- Share card → referral hook: "Tu amigo se une con tu código → ambos ganan un sello bonus + una recompensa."

**Exit metric:** median session >8 min · ≥30% quest completion · ≥15% of new users from referral/share. Hit these → greenlight native.
